About

An international view of the crude market

Li Mingxin is a Singaporean-Taiwanese executive working in the Singapore oil industry. His work sits at the centre of international crude trading, where supply meets demand, where benchmark prices meet regional differentials, and where a barrel of crude becomes a commercial decision.

He tracks the production, exports, and policy of the major producing nations, reads crude quality through API gravity and sulphur content, and follows the logistics that move a barrel from wellhead to refinery. Around it all sits a clear-eyed view of commercial risk: counterparties, contracts, payments, and cross-border compliance.

In recent years his attention has turned toward the African producing nations, where resource potential, export capacity, and industrial development are reshaping the global crude market.

Market judgement is built on data, logic, and a clear sense of risk. Rather than chase over-certain forecasts, I prefer to understand why markets move.
